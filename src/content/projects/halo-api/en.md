## APIs and Theater films {#theater}

halo_api is a Rust library for Halo Infinite APIs and offline parsing of Theater film files. The packet inspector above shows decoded data from a recorded match.

The [Theater reverse-engineering post](https://spencer.imbleau.com/blog/ai/rust/2026/09/14/halo-infinite-theater-format-astra.html) describes the investigation, from controlled recordings to checking a reconstructed replay against gameplay.
